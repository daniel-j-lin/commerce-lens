# Participant consent script

We are evaluating the CommerceLens product, not evaluating you. We will ask
you to complete several ordinary ecommerce-analysis tasks using synthetic data.

With your permission, we may record your prompts, actions, product outputs, and
timestamps as research evidence. Recording is optional. You may decline
recording and still participate, and you may stop at any time without giving a
reason.

The datasets are synthetic. Please do not enter personal information,
customer information, credentials, secrets, or confidential employer data.
We will use an anonymous participant ID in the session record.

Before we begin, do you consent to participate?  Yes / No

Do you consent to screen or session recording?  Yes / No
